slot or successful Open/Cancel smoke test does not mean its whole renderer was extracted.

and intentional legacy/developer remnants. Do not invent a product dialog to satisfy a stale
roadmap name. [S02, S29]

The next Workbench integration slice may be large because it touches multiple native owners;
it must preserve exact definition versions, admissions and recovery, and must not be treated
as permission to refactor the Processes runtime itself. Whole-Workbench completion requires
that final slice and census, not merely this Operators sandbox.
