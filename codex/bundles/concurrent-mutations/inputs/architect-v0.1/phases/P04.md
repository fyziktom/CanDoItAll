# P04 · One governed decision-basis and approval pilot

Status: **Proposed — not started or validated.**

## Goal
Protect a real planning operation against stale inputs and superseded intent, beyond target-row concurrency.

## Dependencies and entry evidence
Dependencies: P03.

- P03 supports the required precise intentions; policy owner defines material inputs and takeover scope.

## Included changes
- Bind exact proposal, mandatory Projects/Workbench inputs and set-membership witness to the original runtime invocation.
- Validate bounded source basis under actual publication protection using typed owner ports.
- Bind approval to the reviewed proposal/basis/authority and reject material drift.
- Implement explicit takeover/override semantics for the pilot and reject late retired attempts at the owner.
- Bound semantic replans and distinguish them from technical retries/outcome lookup.

## Explicit exclusions
- No claim of proof of model reasoning.
- No collecting every read in every chat as a dependency.
- No platform-wide process/checkpoint migration yet.

## Exit gates
- Objective change, phantom task insertion, revoked claim and stale approval tests pass with no partial effects.
- Actual governed tool-to-owner roundtrip and a human intervention UI journey are validated.
- Replan budget, basis limits and source protection level are documented and measured.
