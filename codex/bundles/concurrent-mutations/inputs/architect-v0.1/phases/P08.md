Status: **Proposed — not started or validated.**

## Goal
Publish an accurate coverage contract with lifecycle/runbook/performance evidence, and optimize only demonstrated overhead.

## Dependencies and entry evidence
Dependencies: P03, P05, P06, P07.

- Selected adoption scope is frozen; all required predecessor slices and evidence are complete.

## Included changes
- Reconcile all selected writer inventories, source drift, approved decisions, risks and residual unsupported paths.
- Run documented release/merge/frozen-checkpoint canonical suites and named platform/browser/live/provider lanes as required by actual impact.
- Complete migration/backup/restore/retention and mixed-version operational rehearsals.
- Tune query batching/lock lifetime/payload/revision scope only when measurement and concurrency proofs agree.

## Explicit exclusions
- No marking skipped or unavailable lanes green.
- No unsafe feature flag disabling target or authority checks to hit a latency budget.
- No claim all unselected modules were migrated.

## Exit gates
- Current source, tests, evaluated graph and performance reports agree.
- Deployment/rollback rehearsal preserves enforced writer boundary and operation evidence.
- Remaining risks are specifically accepted with owner and scope; zero undocumented enabled bypasses in advertised coverage.
