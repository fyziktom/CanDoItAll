# P06 · Storage, external delivery and projection integration

Status: **Proposed — not started or validated.**

## Goal
Carry valid intentions and honest outcomes through selected non-SQL boundaries without inventing universal atomicity.

## Dependencies and entry evidence
Dependencies: P04, P05.

- P04 semantics and the relevant P05 owner slice complete; other unrelated P05 waves need not block. Provider capabilities verified.

## Included changes
- Audit actual storage drivers and existing placement/tool receipts; classify conditional replacement and external-edit support.
- Bind external deliveries to proposal/authority and stable provider request identities where supported.
- Implement guarded narrow compensation and explicit uncertain-outcome recovery.
- Add durable projection/outbox work only for selected proven needs; define ordering, backlog, retention and deletion tombstones.

## Explicit exclusions
- No general distributed transaction or exactly-once guarantee.
- No external network/byte work inside a retriable database transaction.

## Exit gates
- Driver/provider-specific failure, timeout-after-accept, external-edit and compensation races pass.
- Every selected external boundary declares its real guarantee and recovery path.
- Backlog/receipt retention and operational limits have measured capacity evidence.

## Performance review
Required workload IDs: PERF-07, PERF-09, PERF-10. See [performance scenarios](../registers/performance_scenarios.json). Measurements remain unset until executed.

## Tests and downstream consumers
The [test catalogue](../docs/21-test-catalogue.md) and [mutation inventory](../registers/mutation_inventory.json) carry this phase ID. Discover actual current test assemblies, filters and counts before implementation. Updating a shared contract also invalidates affected UI/API/tool adapters, serialization snapshots, import/migration mappings and boundary tests even when their files were not directly edited.

## Safe stopping state
Selected external effects have explicit guarantees. Unsupported external CAS/idempotency remains visible rather than silently emulated.

## Residual risks and integration notes
Remote services may not support sufficient dedupe or lookup. Operator recovery can be a required product outcome, not a hidden retry.
