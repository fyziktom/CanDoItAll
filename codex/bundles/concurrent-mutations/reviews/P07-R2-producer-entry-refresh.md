## Smallest useful vertical and its dependencies

Implement the existing native project-backed Process launch → actual Agent executor → bounded Workbench task-plan owner before adding Process → Workflow delegation. Use one declared project scope and a sequential enrolled decision path. The user-visible result must include saved policy, real provider input from the admitted basis, original approval handling, durable changed-basis review and real owner publication. A settings field, synthetic publication authority or dormant journal is not that result.

The prerequisites are distinct: D1 owner proof; R1d actual MAF/tool dispatcher and public-client cutover; C4's real provider-origin admission, schema-six state and versioned work-accounting contract; and a reviewed measured publication-exclusion strategy. D1 itself remains interactive schema five. `RequireSavedJournal` explicitly refuses a background source. R2 must add a validated original background branch and its current Process mutation authority; deleting that refusal is not a compatible extension. Detached execution remains the separate atomic original-catalog admission described by the existing R2 card. A missing Process source must never select detached authority.

For the initial Process vertical, unsupported Workflow, parallel or cross-owner material scopes are rejected at admission. That is an explicit capability boundary, not a replacement for P07's eventual Process/Workflow coverage. Do not advertise pause or replan until its real durable action path exists; requesting an unsupported response fails before model work, without a Pause→Fail downgrade.

## Persisted lifecycle, without a shared engine coordinator

Use the existing engine's durable owner, with small typed fields/references, not a generic runtime coordinator. SharedKernel already supplies validated `DecisionDataPolicy`, override, capabilities, budget, codec and pure resolver. It does not resolve settings or own authority.

For Process, the SQL run/commit owner retains the decision's original namespace/root/run/launch admission, resolved policy/provenance, immutable generation prefix, current generation, active-work state and references to enrolled steps/children. A step is identified by the original StepInstanceId plus owner-issued semantic generation/step identity, not by its rotating dispatch claim. Bind the exact Agent execution and journal session after reservation; a partial reservation must remain a typed unfinished admission, not an invented child completion. Keep preparation intent immutable and put evolving state in the run owner, not in the saved assignment.

For Workflow, the run/checkpoint owner retains the corresponding binding and actual node occurrence. The occurrence from `WorkflowExecutorExecutionAuditScope` and `input.ExecutionOccurrence` is the correct retry unit. A new occurrence must come from the real backend progression; neither executor retry nor external-response replay increments it to evade a conflict. `PersistentWorkflowStores.RequireFrozenAuthority` protects some original origins today but has no general policy/basis immutability rule: the new rule must cover every admitted governed run, not rely on a project-origin conditional accidentally protecting it.

Reuse the C4 state shape locally: persist stable step identity in `CapturingBasis` before awaiting a read; exact CAS attaches the immutable basis and actual provider origin into `PreparedInput`; durable proposal/approval precedes effect entry; known committed and uncertain outcomes survive cancellation and cleanup failure. A failed or cancelled capture leaves explicit retired/incomplete evidence and no provider claim. The engines own their transitions and storage; Workbench receives only its bounded source/basis/command and held authority capability.

`RunSnapshot` retains one declared basis per decision generation across enrolled steps. `StepSnapshot` captures once at admission of a new logical step, including its original scope and factual input. Retries, approval waits, callback delivery, claim rotation and same-step recovery retain that basis. A later step may capture new facts, but immutable launch variables, definition/version and previous provider output remain their original evidence. Never relabel old factual text as a new snapshot. Step overrides cannot weaken an enclosing RunSnapshot scope; unsupported cross-scope composition is rejected.

At initial RunSnapshot admission, capture the declared scope before dependent provider work and bind it in the durable original decision. At StepSnapshot launch, persist policy now but defer business-fact capture until the actual step admission. Resolving every future step's facts in `BuildAssignments` would implement the wrong mode. Capture and external/provider work occur outside publication locks; final owner publication still enforces current target, lifecycle, mandatory material witnesses and current authority.

## Refresh, replan, approvals and late children

The minimal actions are typed Cancel and Refresh on exact displayed decision ID/generation/owner revision, plus automatic replan only under an explicit positive persisted allowance and supported producer capability. A Process `Waiting` state or Workflow checkpoint alone does not prove such an action exists. A no-SDK-approval planning pause needs its own owner action; it must not fabricate a tool approval or successful assistant output.

Before generation CAS, reconcile every enrolled old child that may have entered an effect. Unknown commit, unresolved checkpoint, source-lease release uncertainty or activity-finalization failure blocks replacement. Commit-before-supersession remains accepted history; supersession-before-commit refuses the stale child. Cancellation must preserve late accepted/uncertain results and the exact old approvals. The new generation uses a new basis/proposal digest and new approval where required; old approval IDs/response operations and original operation caller keys remain attached only to old intent.

For Process, extend the existing root-guarded commit perimeter, including dispatch/cancel/recovery and assignment repair, before claiming generation exclusion. `EfProcessWorkflowDispatchAuthority` has a separate root-locked current-claim and retained continuation path; its waiting-parent/complete-event-range/StepReady guards remain. Do not reinterpret a new generation as that old continuation. The mapped Workflow material binding must add decision identity while `WorkflowProcessDispatchBinding.HasSameIntent` continues to ignore ordinary claim-token rotation. This requires a versioned fingerprint/domain and old read compatibility.

Cross-file Agent admission plus Process SQL is not an atomic transaction. Freeze the exact acquisition/release order against the selected C4 file/source guard before source release. No SQL root holder may recursively acquire an organization catalog guard if another path holds that guard while waiting for the same SQL root. Original source and target owners must both be current at effect publication; a read-only historical claim or process-local host generation cannot supply mutation authority.

## Restart and work accounting

Carry the C4 amendment's separate active-work ledger, not `now - run.CreatedAt` and not a rule that every Waiting state excludes work. D1 WaitingOnTool can still contain active preflight. Open work before capture/preflight/provider/owner activity; close it atomically only at an explicit durable human wait/pause or terminal transition, preserving cumulative charge across generations and all child retries. Original decision age, basis age and absolute owner-operation horizon remain separate. C3 v1 SQL wall-clock policy is unchanged; an active-work publisher needs its later versioned source/command interpretation.

The first sequential vertical can keep the enclosing decision active while its child is active and close it only when the durable parent/child wait transition agrees; it must enforce both enclosing and child allowances without resetting either. Parallel nested accounting is not established by this sequential design. It needs an explicit owner rule for overlapping intervals and concurrent charging before activation, not a sum or discount invented by a UI.

Same-host intervals use trusted monotonic time and bounded checked arithmetic. Closed-wait resume still requires actual current mutation admission. An open interval lost on crash cannot be assigned a new monotonic origin or zero charge. The proposed first version refuses active-work mutation resume until trusted conservative settlement is implemented; alternatively exhausting the remaining budget must be an explicit versioned owner outcome with preserved effects. A separate new decision requires reconciling/terminating the original and explicit admission, not resetting its generation budget. Clock rollback/overflow remains a typed refusal with cancellation/receipt inspection available. Historical receipt review does not clear any of these restrictions.
