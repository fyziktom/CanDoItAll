## Environmental execution

Do not use normal app port 5032, retained databases, real mail/payment endpoints or unowned
Docker services. Use task-owned projects, safe files, local scripted external responses and
actual production owners. A headless publish must start/restart outside the source checkout.
Keep Windows/Linux/macOS claims platform-specific; unavailable macOS is not a passing run.
No test timeout, skip/quarantine or permission expansion is allowed merely for closure.

Live groups are conditional on a new explicit authorization, not exempted from accounting.
Keep the original 40-reservation journal immutable. Record new requests, their cap, actual
provider attempts, safe terminal states and owner effects independently. A disabled gate or
rehearsal remains NOT_RUN/REHEARSAL even if the runner reports Passed.
