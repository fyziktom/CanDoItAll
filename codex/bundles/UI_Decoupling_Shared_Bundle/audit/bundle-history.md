## What changes in v4

Keep the good v3 architecture, remove dated concrete defect claims unless freshly verified, and give each new executor one current shared companion plus one coherent assignment. Do not recursively copy old bundles. Do not translate an old report into current passing evidence. A small startup correction is followed by the assigned full family in the same run; it is not a new stopping point.

The active shared directory is upgraded deliberately. Historical child directories and their embedded v3 copies remain historical inputs, not mutable result stores. New or modified engineering content is English. Current generic product guidance belongs in `docs`; demo-specific setups and rehearsal records belong in `CanDoItAll.Demos` under current repository rules.
