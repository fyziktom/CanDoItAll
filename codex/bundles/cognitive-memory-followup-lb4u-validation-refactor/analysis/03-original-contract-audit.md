# Original Contract Audit

## Non-Negotiable Invariants

- Raw source items and provenance remain the truth source.
- Canonical memories are curated or reviewed representations, not arbitrary direct writes.
- Projections, embeddings, and vector stores are rebuildable views.
- Generated summaries must keep provenance and review state.
- Probing and professor review may propose or assess, but they must not directly mutate canonical truth.
- Distributed workers can process jobs and return results; the central service applies policy and persistence.
- Epistemic drive identifies gaps and proposals, not unreviewed facts.

## Required Capability Families

- Source ingestion from project structure, process/workflow runtime, files, links, and future external connectors.
- Taxonomy, workspace attention, temporal replay, signal ledger, score geometry, and neuro-foundation layers.
- Recall orchestration across lexical, vector, graph, temporal, and score channels.
- Consolidation/dreaming with candidate review.
- Human review decisions and traceable audit.
- Probing sessions, turn feedback, regression probes, and calibration.
- Answer gate and professor-review style critique.
- Cross-project promotion and reusable knowledge.
- Distributed background work and settings/governance.

## Follow-Up Implication

This bundle must validate the implementation as a learning system. Merely having endpoints, entities, and tests is not enough. The LB4U staged import is the behavioral proof: memory must keep LB4U-specific facts, source chunks, decisions, procedures, risks, and reusable planning knowledge separate but connected.
