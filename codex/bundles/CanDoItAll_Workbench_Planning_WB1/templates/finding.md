# Finding ID and title

## Origin and status
Reviewed source/commit, newly reproduced versus source-derived, existing versus introduced,
severity and affected safe/hazardous paths. Do not infer causation from timing alone.

## Exact reproduction
Original target/admission/profile, call path, controlled waits and expected versus observed
outcome. Distinguish draft/UI, committed native record, external effect and read-back.

## Impact and boundary
Affected owners/consumers, retained identities and source of authority. Include the original
and secondary failure where compensation or cleanup is involved. Omit credentials/private text.

## Repair and validation
Smallest correct owner change, compatibility concerns, native regression and final commands.
For a complex blocker give a precise follow-up plan; mapping is not repair. Preserve failures.
