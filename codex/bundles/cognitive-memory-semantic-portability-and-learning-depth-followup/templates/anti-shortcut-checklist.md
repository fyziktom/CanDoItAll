# Anti-shortcut Checklist

- No production branch may check a unit test name, fixture title, or exact example sentence from this bundle.
- No subbundle may close by renaming labels without changing behavior.
- No validator change may only check for prose labels.
- No professor mastery transition may be based only on text saying `mastered` or `internalized`.
- No dream validation may approve a claim only because it has enough overlapping tokens.
- No cross-project mode may be implemented by same-project clusters with a cross-project title.
