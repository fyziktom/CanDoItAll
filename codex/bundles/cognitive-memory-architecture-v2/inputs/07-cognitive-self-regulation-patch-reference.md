## Imported Inputs

The patch identifies a real gap in the v2 bundle: workspace, attention, salience, probing calibration, score geometry, and the metamemory answer gate exist, but they are distributed mechanisms. The bundle needs an explicit control layer that coordinates those mechanisms as self-regulation.

The imported self-regulation requirements are:

- durable self-model and domain competence profiles,
- known failure patterns and humility triggers,
- self-regulation assessment before important answers, tools, workflows, probes, reviews, and mutations,
- first-class answer posture decisions,
- calibration health aggregates and binned calibration metrics,
- professor review as challenge/audit input,
- post-outcome feedback into calibration, prediction errors, salience, replay, probing, review, and self-model update proposals.

## Integration Decision

Self-Regulation is not a new truth source, personality layer, emotional simulation, or autonomous consciousness model. It is modeled as calibrated agency under epistemic uncertainty.

The v2 bundle now treats Self-Regulation as a control layer after probing-core calibration evidence exists and before the metamemory answer gate is trusted for user/agent answer rendering. The answer gate remains the final answer-time boundary and may become stricter than self-regulation, but it cannot become looser without a new score-geometry trace.

## Hard Constraints Preserved

- Self-model records cannot mutate canonical memory.
- Professor review output is evidence/challenge/review input only.
- Generated summaries, salience, prediction errors, probing feedback, and self-regulation outcomes cannot become source truth directly.
- Score geometry remains mandatory for self-regulation assessment, posture selection, calibration health, and professor-review routing.
- Access policy, redaction, mutation authority, review policy, and source truth hierarchy remain upstream of every self-regulation action.
