# LedgerLens Compliance SaaS

LedgerLens is a B2B SaaS concept for finance teams preparing revenue recognition evidence. The product does not replace accounting judgement. Its safer position is continuous evidence completeness, policy drift detection, review routing, and audit-pack generation.

The target segment is controller-led SaaS companies with contract changes, usage-based pricing, multiple legal entities, and an annual audit cycle. The wedge is the work that is tedious and auditable: collecting contract metadata, invoices, payment events, product usage, and policy snapshots, then explaining where the evidence does not match the policy.

The business model should avoid per-seat-only pricing. Audit complexity is driven by entities, transactions, contract variation, and external auditor access. A base platform fee plus entity and transaction tiers better matches value and support cost.

Key risks are trust, liability, integration fatigue, and sensitive financial data. Logs must avoid contract values and bank references. The design partner package should include a written limitation statement that the customer remains the accounting decision owner.
