# Cutover rehearsal checklist

**Key:** `cutover-rehearsal-checklist`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** release-readiness-and-deployment  
**Owner role key:** `change-manager`  
**Phase:** rehearsal

## Summary
Checks whether the cutover or deployment path was rehearsed enough to trust the event plan.

## Entry criteria
A significant release or cutover event is nearing execution.

## Exit criteria
Rehearsal or dry-run evidence is sufficient for the declared risk level.

## Checks
- Cutover sequence has been walked through or rehearsed.
- Hold points and rollback triggers are understood.
- Watch roster is assigned.
- Support and communication escalation paths are tested.

## Evidence expectations
- Cutover watch roster
- Rehearsal notes
