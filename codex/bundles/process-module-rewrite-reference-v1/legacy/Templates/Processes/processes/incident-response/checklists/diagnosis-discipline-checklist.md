# Diagnosis discipline checklist

**Key:** `diagnosis-discipline-checklist`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** incident-response  
**Owner role key:** `resolver`  
**Phase:** Diagnosis

## Summary
Keeps incident diagnosis evidence-led and protects against destructive or unsupported mitigation guesses.

## Entry criteria
The incident has been triaged and diagnosis is underway.

## Exit criteria
Diagnosis notes distinguish hypotheses, evidence, and approved mitigation clearly.

## Checks
- Current hypotheses are labeled clearly.
- Evidence sources are cited.
- Potentially destructive mitigations are flagged for approval.
- Rollback or reversal options are noted where applicable.

## Evidence expectations
- Diagnosis evidence pack.
- Containment or mitigation note.
- Approval escalation if needed.
