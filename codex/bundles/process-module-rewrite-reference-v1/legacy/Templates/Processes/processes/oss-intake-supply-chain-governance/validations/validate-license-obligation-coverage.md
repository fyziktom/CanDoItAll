# Validate license obligation coverage

**Key:** `validate-license-obligation-coverage`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** oss-intake-supply-chain-governance  
**Owner role key:** `license-counsel`  
**Gate:** component-intake  
**Failure severity:** Error

## Summary
Confirms all relevant license obligations were identified and assigned.

## Pass criteria
Component obligations are explicit and mapped to a concrete delivery or compliance action where needed.

## Fail criteria
License interpretation remains ambiguous or obligations have no owner.

## Escalation rule
Escalate to compliance steward and sponsoring manager before component approval.
