# License counsel

**Key:** `license-counsel`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** oss-intake-supply-chain-governance  
**Preferred executor:** person  
**Preferred project role:** Reviewer  
**Seniority:** Senior OSS legal/compliance counsel  
**Minimum years in primary discipline:** 7  
**Minimum years in software delivery:** 9

## Summary
Specialist reviewer for open-source license obligations, incompatibilities, and exception framing.

## Purpose
Ensure component use decisions reflect actual license obligations and approved exception logic.

## Staffing intent
A legal or licensing specialist focused on OSS usage governance.

## Snapshot summary
Specialist reviewer for open-source license obligations, incompatibilities, and exception framing.

## Domain tags
open-source-licenses, legal, policy

## Knowledge requirements
- Deep knowledge of common open-source licenses, notice obligations, reciprocity triggers, and distribution models.
- Ability to interpret license implications in the actual product delivery context.
- Knowledge of when engineering or product assumptions create legal risk.
- Understanding of exception and approval patterns for non-standard component use.
- Ability to separate opinion, precedent, and mandatory restriction clearly.
- Knowledge of how license obligations interact with third-party distribution and customer commitments.

## Experience requirements
- Has reviewed OSS component usage for a commercial product or enterprise environment.
- Has handled license incompatibility or obligation-escalation scenarios.
- Has written or approved a licensing position that guided engineering action.
- Has worked with compliance stewards and engineers on OSS policy execution.
- Has helped define reusable policy patterns for repeated component intake.

## Decision rights
- Approve, reject, or conditionally approve license acceptability.
- Require notices, attribution, or exception handling before component adoption.
- Escalate unresolved incompatibility to accountable governance owners.
- Define whether a component can move forward with conditions.

## Owned artifacts
- License review note
- OSS exception request
- Obligation summary

## Collaboration expectations
- Work with compliance steward, SBOM curator, and engineers.
- Explain consequences in delivery-usable language.
- Review policy questions early enough to avoid wasted implementation effort.
- Document precedent carefully for future reuse.

## Anti-patterns
- Treating popular components as automatically safe from a license standpoint.
- Giving vague legal caution without a usable recommendation.
- Approving exceptions without required follow-up obligations.
- Waiting until release to ask how the component is actually distributed.

## Fitness evidence
- Clear licensing decisions consumed by delivery teams.
- Reduced late-stage OSS licensing surprises.
- Documented obligation patterns reused across component reviews.
- Audit or legal review confidence in the role’s output.
