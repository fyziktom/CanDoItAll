# Onboarding program manager

**Key:** `onboarding-program-manager`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** customer-onboarding  
**Preferred executor:** person  
**Preferred project role:** Manager  
**Seniority:** Senior implementation or onboarding program management  
**Minimum years in primary discipline:** 5  
**Minimum years in software delivery:** 7

## Summary
Cross-functional owner for onboarding milestone orchestration, readiness tracking, and dependency closure.

## Purpose
Turn the onboarding promise into an executable, customer-visible plan with explicit ownership on both sides.

## Staffing intent
A delivery manager specialized in onboarding and implementation programs.

## Snapshot summary
Cross-functional owner for onboarding milestone orchestration, readiness tracking, and dependency closure.

## Domain tags
onboarding, implementation, milestone-management

## Knowledge requirements
- Ability to define onboarding phases, exit criteria, and ownership on the customer and vendor side.
- Knowledge of common onboarding blockers such as access, data, training, security review, and stakeholder alignment.
- Understanding of implementation planning, cadence, and escalation in customer-facing contexts.
- Ability to assess whether a milestone is truly complete or merely discussed.
- Knowledge of cutover preparation and early-life support planning.
- Ability to keep internal and customer timelines aligned without masking risk.

## Experience requirements
- Has run a structured customer onboarding or implementation program.
- Has recovered an onboarding plan after scope, staffing, or customer-readiness drift.
- Has managed milestone or checklist-based customer governance.
- Has coordinated technical and non-technical contributors through go-live preparation.
- Has handled executive or commercial escalation tied to onboarding delay.

## Decision rights
- Approve milestone plan and readiness exit criteria.
- Escalate dependency slippage on either customer or internal side.
- Declare whether onboarding is ready to move into cutover/go-live preparation.
- Insist on explicit owner and due date for every open onboarding blocker.

## Owned artifacts
- Onboarding milestone plan
- Readiness tracker
- Cutover readiness note

## Collaboration expectations
- Coordinate with customer success lead, product owner, and solution consultant.
- Keep the customer and internal team on one shared plan.
- Surface unresolved blockers early rather than at the milestone review.
- Document decisions and action ownership after every checkpoint.

## Anti-patterns
- Treating a verbal agreement as a completed onboarding milestone.
- Optimizing for stakeholder comfort instead of truthful readiness signals.
- Collapsing customer and internal responsibilities into vague shared ownership.
- Skipping explicit cutover or support-readiness planning.

## Fitness evidence
- Milestone trackers with clear completion logic and owners.
- Examples of recovered onboarding plans.
- Customer-facing governance notes with actionable follow-up.
- Reduced surprise during go-live transitions.
