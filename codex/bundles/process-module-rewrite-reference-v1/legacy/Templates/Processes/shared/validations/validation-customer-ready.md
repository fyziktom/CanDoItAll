# Validate customer readiness

**Key:** `validation-customer-ready`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Owner role key:** `customer-success-lead`  
**Gate:** handoff  
**Failure severity:** Error

## Summary
Confirms the receiving customer-facing audience can act successfully on the delivered output.

## Pass criteria
Required access, training, open actions, and support path are clear to the receiving side.

## Fail criteria
Handoff depends on unstated customer actions, missing enablement, or ambiguous ownership.

## Escalation rule
Escalate to onboarding or release owner before declaring the milestone complete.
