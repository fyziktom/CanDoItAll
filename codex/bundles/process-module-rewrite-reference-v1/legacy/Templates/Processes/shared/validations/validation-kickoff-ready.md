# Kickoff ready

**Key:** `validation-kickoff-ready`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Owner role key:** `kickoff-lead`  
**Gate:** Kickoff approval  
**Failure severity:** Error

## Summary
Blocks kickoff approval when owners, milestones, or critical dependencies are still unclear.

## Pass criteria
Kickoff artifacts identify accountable owners, milestones, risks, and next actions clearly enough to begin execution.

## Fail criteria
Kickoff would close while core owners, milestones, or risks remain vague or unowned.

## Escalation rule
Hold kickoff approval and escalate unresolved ownership or readiness gaps.
