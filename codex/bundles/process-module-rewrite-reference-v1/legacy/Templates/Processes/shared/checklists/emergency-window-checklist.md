# Emergency window checklist

**Key:** `emergency-window-checklist`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Owner role key:** `release-approver`  
**Phase:** Emergency approval

## Summary
Confirms the emergency release window remains bounded, reversible, and adequately staffed before approval is granted.

## Entry criteria
Blast radius and emergency validation evidence are available.

## Exit criteria
The emergency window has explicit start conditions, rollback triggers, watch owners, and communication coverage.

## Checks
- Blast radius is explicit and bounded.
- Emergency validation evidence names what was and was not tested.
- Rollback trigger and stop conditions are explicit.
- Watch owner and communication owner are named.

## Evidence expectations
- Emergency window approval record.
- Rollback trigger sheet.
- Emergency bridge log.
