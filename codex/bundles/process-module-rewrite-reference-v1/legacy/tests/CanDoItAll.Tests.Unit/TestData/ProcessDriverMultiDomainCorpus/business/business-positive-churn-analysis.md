# SB043 Business Positive Fixture

Requirement: Explain customer churn risk for the enterprise onboarding segment using only supplied evidence.

Evidence: Interview summaries from the renewal team show that customers with delayed admin setup are more likely to request an extension before renewal.

Analysis: The deliverable ties the recommendation to the supplied interview evidence and avoids introducing new assumptions.

Conclusion: Prioritize onboarding reminders and make the risk threshold explicit in the review checklist.
