The historical 27 edit/undo cycles and recorded times remain unchanged. The original harness labeled any main-frame `framenavigated` event as a browser reload. That Playwright event also occurs during same-document Blazor enhanced navigation. The recorded 18 navigation events therefore do not establish 18 new document loads. The nine static-update classifications are unaffected by this specific observation defect.

The [separate successor diagnostic](../../UI_AgentCatalog_Harden_02_Reload_Evidence_Bundle/proof/diagnostic-table.md) records document identity, managed generation, SDK message types, completed enhanced navigation and final visible predicates. Its six refresh-on edits used same-document enhanced navigation; its six refresh-off edits used managed Hot Reload without navigation. No new document or process restart occurred in that new bounded diagnostic. This finding does not retroactively measure the old runs or change their original result files. The old timing values remain observed edit-to-visible values under their documented protocol and limitations; the old reload labels must be read with this correction.
