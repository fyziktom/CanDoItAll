These are acceptance groups, not prescribed test classes or duplicate suite runs.
Use exact current owning tests and add the missing controlled cases. Reuse one
attempt across relevant groups without counting its cases twice. The concrete
sequences and native oracles are in [APPLICATION_JOURNEYS.md](APPLICATION_JOURNEYS.md).
