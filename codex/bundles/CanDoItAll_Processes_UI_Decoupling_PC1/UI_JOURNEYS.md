## Evidence and faults

Keep controlled faults inside test/fixture composition; do not add production fault endpoints, global permission bypasses or demo identities. Screenshots establish presentation at a state, not native commit/authorization. Pair them with relevant owner queries/IDs/content/dispatch counts and the exact candidate. Retain original failed attempts and reruns. A skipped or closed live gate is not a successful live journey.
