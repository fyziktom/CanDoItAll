Notes:

- Workflow projection consumes explicit output mapping before assigning `ArtifactExpectationId`.
- Ambiguous workflow artifacts are recorded as `Other` and cannot satisfy a process artifact expectation through title fallback.
- Subprocess projection consumes explicit child expectation mappings and records projection diagnostics when ambiguous.
- Tests cover same-kind cases where the old title/name heuristic would choose the wrong output.
