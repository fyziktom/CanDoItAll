# QA Prompt

Validate the project-structure canvas route in a headed browser. Review screenshots for these questions: Are the enlarged marker glyphs readable without enlarging the badge circle? Does the floating toolbox stay fully visible and layered correctly above the canvas? Is the selection target obvious? After applying at least two markers to one node, do both markers remain visible on the node and in the selection summary? Reopen the owning subbundle if any of those answers is no.
