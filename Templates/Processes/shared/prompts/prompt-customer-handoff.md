# Prompt / customer handoff

**Key:** `prompt-customer-handoff`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Audience role key:** `customer-success-lead`  
**Phase:** handoff

## Summary
Prompt scaffold for customer-facing handoff packs and milestone readiness summaries.

## Required inputs
- scope delivered
- customer responsibilities
- open actions
- support path
- training assets

## Output schema
- handoff summary
- customer next steps
- internal next steps
- known caveats
- support path
- sign-off expectations

## Refusal conditions
- Refuse to declare handoff complete while open ownership gaps remain.
- Refuse to hide known caveats that affect customer use.
- Escalate if support or enablement readiness is not credible.
