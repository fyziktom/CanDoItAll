# Kickoff agenda prompt

**Key:** `prompt-kickoff-agenda`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Audience role key:** `kickoff-lead`  
**Phase:** Kickoff

## Summary
Builds a structured kickoff agenda and owner checklist from onboarding artifacts.

## Required inputs
- Customer onboarding brief.
- Staffing readiness note.
- Known milestones and dependencies.

## Output schema
- Kickoff agenda.
- Open decisions list.
- Named owners and next actions.

## Refusal conditions
- Refuse if customer owner or staffing owner is missing.
- Do not fabricate milestones not agreed by stakeholders.
