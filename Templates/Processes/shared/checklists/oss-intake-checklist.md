# OSS intake checklist

**Key:** `oss-intake-checklist`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Owner role key:** `compliance-steward`  
**Phase:** oss-intake

## Summary
Open-source intake checklist covering license, provenance, security, and metadata completeness.

## Entry criteria
A new component or dependency is being considered for use.

## Exit criteria
Component decision is explicit, evidence-backed, and reusable later without redoing basic due diligence.

## Checks
- Component identity, version, and source are known.
- License obligations and compatibility were reviewed.
- Security or supply-chain concerns were reviewed proportionally to risk.
- SBOM and provenance expectations are defined.
- Approval conditions or exception logic are explicit.
- Reuse restrictions are documented when evidence is incomplete.

## Evidence expectations
- OSS review note.
- SBOM manifest or gap log.
- License obligation summary.
