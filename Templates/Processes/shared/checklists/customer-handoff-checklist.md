# Customer handoff checklist

**Key:** `customer-handoff-checklist`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Owner role key:** `customer-success-lead`  
**Phase:** handoff

## Summary
Handoff completeness checklist for onboarding, launch, or release communication to downstream users.

## Entry criteria
A customer-facing milestone or onboarding handoff is about to be declared complete.

## Exit criteria
The receiving side has enough information, training, and ownership clarity to proceed without rediscovering basics.

## Checks
- Customer-facing milestone or scope is clearly defined.
- Configuration, access, or prerequisite actions have explicit owners.
- Training, release notes, or usage guidance exist where behavior changed.
- Known caveats and next-step ownership are documented.
- Support path for early-life issues is explicit.
- Customer sign-off or acknowledgment expectation is clear.

## Evidence expectations
- Handoff pack.
- Open actions tracker.
- Customer-facing readiness note.
