# Incident triage discipline checklist

**Key:** `incident-triage-discipline-checklist`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Owner role key:** `triage-lead`  
**Phase:** Incident triage

## Summary
Forces early incident triage to remain explicit, evidence-led, and role-accountable.

## Entry criteria
A credible operational signal or alert has triggered triage.

## Exit criteria
Severity, responders, impact framing, and first actions are recorded explicitly.

## Checks
- Severity is declared or explicitly deferred with rationale.
- Named responders and stakeholders are recorded.
- User or tenant impact framing is explicit.
- First containment or diagnostic action is identified.
- Unsupported assumptions are labeled as hypotheses.

## Evidence expectations
- Incident triage note.
- Responder mobilization record.
- Initial timeline entry.
