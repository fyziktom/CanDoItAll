# AI governance checklist

**Key:** `ai-governance-checklist`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Owner role key:** `ai-safety-reviewer`  
**Phase:** ai-governance

## Summary
AI-specific readiness checklist for model, prompt, evaluation, and human-oversight governance.

## Entry criteria
An AI-assisted workflow or AI-enabled change is proposed or nearing release.

## Exit criteria
Use is bounded, evaluated, monitored, and approved under explicit operating conditions.

## Checks
- Model, prompt, tool, and data provenance are identified.
- Allowed use case and forbidden use cases are explicit.
- Evaluation evidence and failure modes are documented.
- Human review, refusal, and escalation conditions are real and staffed.
- Monitoring or periodic re-validation expectations are defined.
- Approval conditions and expiry logic are documented.

## Evidence expectations
- AI evaluation report.
- Prompt package.
- AI risk approval or exception note.
