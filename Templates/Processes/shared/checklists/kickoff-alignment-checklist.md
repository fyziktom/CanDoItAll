# Kickoff alignment checklist

**Key:** `kickoff-alignment-checklist`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Owner role key:** `kickoff-lead`  
**Phase:** Kickoff

## Summary
Ensures onboarding kickoff closes with explicit owners, milestones, risks, and next actions.

## Entry criteria
Kickoff agenda and pre-read materials have been prepared.

## Exit criteria
Kickoff closes only when owners, risks, milestones, and next actions are explicit and reviewable.

## Checks
- Customer-side and delivery-side owners are named.
- Milestones and near-term sequencing are recorded.
- Risks and dependencies are explicit.
- Open decisions have owners and due dates.
- Next actions are captured with accountable names.

## Evidence expectations
- Kickoff packet.
- Action tracker.
- Kickoff approval record.
