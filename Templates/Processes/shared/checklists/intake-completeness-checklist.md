# Intake completeness checklist

**Key:** `intake-completeness-checklist`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Owner role key:** `product-owner`  
**Phase:** intake

## Summary
Minimal completeness gate before a request is treated as execution-ready rather than conversational.

## Entry criteria
A request has been captured and an accountable owner has been nominated.

## Exit criteria
Scope, owner, desired outcome, constraints, and unresolved assumptions are explicit enough to plan responsibly.

## Checks
- The request source and accountable outcome owner are identified.
- Desired business or operational outcome is stated in measurable language.
- Scope boundary and non-goals are written down.
- Known dependency, compliance, and timing constraints are listed.
- Explicit project-structure requirements remain required unless the source says they are optional or deferred.
- Any missing input is marked as unresolved rather than silently assumed.
- Required review roles are identified proportionally to risk.

## Evidence expectations
- Completed intake brief.
- Named product owner or equivalent accountable owner.
- Evidence of unresolved assumptions captured explicitly.
