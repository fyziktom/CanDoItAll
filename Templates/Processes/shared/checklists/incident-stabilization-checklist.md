# Incident stabilization checklist

**Key:** `incident-stabilization-checklist`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Owner role key:** `incident-commander`  
**Phase:** incident

## Summary
Containment and stabilization checklist for active incidents.

## Entry criteria
An incident is active and response coordination has started.

## Exit criteria
Containment path, ownership, and next review horizon are explicit enough to avoid chaotic response.

## Checks
- Incident severity and current customer impact are stated.
- Incident commander, specialists, and communications owners are assigned.
- Containment goal and immediate decision horizon are explicit.
- Evidence preservation needs are known before destructive action.
- Customer/executive communication path is defined where required.
- Next checkpoint time and required inputs are stated.

## Evidence expectations
- Live incident notes or timeline seed.
- Named responder assignments.
- Containment decision summary.
