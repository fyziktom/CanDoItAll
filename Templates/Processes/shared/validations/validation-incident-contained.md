# Validate incident containment state

**Key:** `validation-incident-contained`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Owner role key:** `incident-commander`  
**Gate:** incident  
**Failure severity:** Error

## Summary
Confirms the incident has a known containment direction, assigned owners, and a factual status baseline.

## Pass criteria
Impact, owners, and next decision horizon are explicit, and no critical action is ownerless.

## Fail criteria
Response is chaotic, impact framing is unstable, or key containment action lacks ownership.

## Escalation rule
Escalate immediately to the senior incident command chain.
