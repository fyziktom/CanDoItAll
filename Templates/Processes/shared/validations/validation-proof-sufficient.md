# Validate implementation proof sufficiency

**Key:** `validation-proof-sufficient`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Owner role key:** `qa-lead`  
**Gate:** verification  
**Failure severity:** Error

## Summary
Confirms implementation evidence is decision-grade for the declared risk level.

## Pass criteria
Evidence is reproducible, coverage scope is explicit, and residual quality risk is stated in actionable language.

## Fail criteria
Evidence is anecdotal, unreproducible, or fails to explain what remains unverified.

## Escalation rule
Escalate to QA lead and release approver; do not promote to release readiness.
