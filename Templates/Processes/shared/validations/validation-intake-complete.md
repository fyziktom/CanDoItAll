# Validate intake completeness

**Key:** `validation-intake-complete`  
**Scope:** shared  
**Process:** shared  
**Owner role key:** `product-owner`  
**Gate:** intake  
**Failure severity:** Error

## Summary
Confirms a request is structured enough for accountable planning and staffing.

## Pass criteria
Owner, outcome, scope boundary, and major constraints are explicit and no unresolved assumption hides a critical decision.

## Fail criteria
Critical decision inputs are missing, owner is unclear, or acceptance semantics are still implicit.

## Escalation rule
Escalate to the delivery manager and block planning-heavy work until the intake brief is corrected.
