# Architecture decision readiness checklist

**Key:** `decision-readiness-checklist`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** architecture-decision-governance  
**Owner role key:** `governance-facilitator`  
**Phase:** decision-readiness

## Summary
Checks whether an architecture proposal is mature enough for a governance decision.

## Entry criteria
A proposal is about to enter formal architecture governance.

## Exit criteria
Decision participants can answer the core question with evidence instead of re-discovering context live.

## Checks
- Decision question is explicit.
- Relevant domain owners were consulted or intentionally not needed.
- Options and consequences are documented.
- Implementation pressure or deadline is visible.

## Evidence expectations
- Decision brief
- ADR draft or equivalent
