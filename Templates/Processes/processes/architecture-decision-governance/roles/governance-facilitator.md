# Governance facilitator

**Key:** `governance-facilitator`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** architecture-decision-governance  
**Preferred executor:** person  
**Preferred project role:** Manager  
**Seniority:** Senior PMO, architecture operations, or governance lead  
**Minimum years in primary discipline:** 5  
**Minimum years in software delivery:** 7

## Summary
Facilitator for architecture decision workflow discipline, records, and follow-through.

## Purpose
Keep architecture governance lightweight but rigorous by ensuring the right decision, evidence, and follow-up happen at the right time.

## Staffing intent
A governance-capable facilitator who understands enough architecture to structure the process without dominating the decision.

## Snapshot summary
Facilitator for architecture decision workflow discipline, records, and follow-through.

## Domain tags
facilitation, decision-records, governance

## Knowledge requirements
- Ability to structure review cadence, decision input, and follow-up tracking for architecture forums.
- Knowledge of what makes an ADR or design decision truly decision-ready.
- Understanding of stakeholder alignment patterns and unresolved-risk escalation.
- Ability to detect when discussion is circling without explicit decision framing.
- Knowledge of traceability and retention needs for governance artifacts.
- Ability to keep governance proportional to the cost of the decision.

## Experience requirements
- Has facilitated design or governance forums with multiple stakeholders.
- Has maintained ADR or design review pipelines and follow-up tracking.
- Has converted vague design discussion into explicit decision outcomes.
- Has pushed incomplete proposals back for refinement without adding bureaucracy.
- Has closed the loop on architecture action items after review.

## Decision rights
- Decide whether a proposal is ready for formal review.
- Require decision record completeness and explicit action ownership.
- Escalate when architecture risk is unresolved but implementation pressure is mounting.
- Define the minimal review footprint for low versus high impact decisions.

## Owned artifacts
- Review agenda
- Decision record checklist
- Action follow-up log

## Collaboration expectations
- Coordinate between architects, domain owners, and delivery managers.
- Keep review participants aligned on the current question and exit criteria.
- Preserve decision records and action traces cleanly.
- Improve the governance loop based on repeated friction patterns.

## Anti-patterns
- Turning every design question into a heavyweight forum item.
- Allowing review meetings to end without a decision or owned next step.
- Confusing facilitation authority with design authority.
- Treating documentation as the outcome instead of the decision.

## Fitness evidence
- Governance records with clear outcomes and owners.
- Reduced decision drift or repeated review loops.
- Stakeholder trust in review readiness signals.
- Action closure rates after facilitated governance sessions.
