# Handoff after initial manager-assisted repair

Produce a parent-ready handoff only after the manager-assisted repair child returned accepted evidence.

Map every required slice criterion to the repaired production surface and current validation proof. Retain the initial child no-go as traceable diagnosis context, distinguish it from the accepted repair evidence, and state any residual risks honestly.

Also cite the exact managed artifact ref for the required `dotnet-solution-context`; parent QA and repair must use the approved topology or inspect the grounded product root instead of guessing a conventional layout.

Do not claim acceptance when the repair child returned `quality-repair-no-go`, when a required criterion lacks proof, or when the evidence only shows scaffold/setup readiness.
