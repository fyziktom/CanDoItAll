# Account owner

**Key:** `account-owner`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** customer-onboarding  
**Preferred executor:** person  
**Preferred project role:** CustomerContact  
**Seniority:** Senior account executive or customer success owner  
**Minimum years in primary discipline:** 5  
**Minimum years in software delivery:** 6

## Summary
Commercial owner for onboarding priority, stakeholder alignment, and acceptance timing.

## Purpose
Own the customer-facing commercial context for onboarding and keep success criteria explicit before staffing or kickoff commitments are made.

## Staffing intent
A customer-facing owner who can confirm commercial priority, named stakeholders, and outcome expectations without bypassing delivery feasibility checks.

## Snapshot summary
Commercial owner for onboarding priority, stakeholder alignment, and acceptance timing.

## Domain tags
customer-onboarding, commercial-alignment, stakeholder-management, acceptance

## Knowledge requirements
- Ability to structure onboarding or delivery milestones in customer-comprehensible language.
- Knowledge of customer stakeholder mapping, expectation management, and handoff design.
- Understanding of adoption blockers such as access, training, configuration, and unclear ownership.
- Ability to connect technical delivery status to externally meaningful outcomes and risks.
- Knowledge of escalation patterns when internal delivery and customer timelines diverge.
- Ability to document readiness, open actions, and responsibilities without ambiguity.

## Experience requirements
- Has led customer onboarding, implementation, or adoption work with multiple stakeholder groups.
- Has managed expectation resets when internal readiness lagged external commitments.
- Has coordinated technical and non-technical parties through a go-live or enablement plan.
- Has captured and followed through on customer-raised blockers or training gaps.
- Has supported post-launch stabilization or early-life support.

## Decision rights
- Approve readiness of customer-facing communications and adoption handoff.
- Escalate when internal uncertainty would create misleading customer commitments.
- Require explicit ownership for customer action items before declaring onboarding complete.
- Define whether a customer-facing milestone is truly achieved.

## Owned artifacts
- Onboarding plan
- Customer handoff checklist
- Training readiness pack
- Open actions tracker

## Collaboration expectations
- Work with product, delivery, and solution consultants to keep customer commitments real.
- Coordinate with service and release owners on go-live readiness and support expectations.
- Represent customer adoption risk in internal governance forums.
- Document open actions so the customer is not forced to rediscover missing work.

## Anti-patterns
- Promising dates or capabilities not anchored in the internal plan.
- Declaring onboarding complete while training, access, or ownership gaps remain open.
- Using soft language that hides customer-facing risk.
- Treating customer questions as noise instead of leading indicators of unclear delivery.

## Fitness evidence
- Customer handoff artifacts with explicit owners and dates.
- Examples of expectation resets handled transparently.
- Evidence of adoption or onboarding milestones achieved with low rework.
- Positive internal feedback that the role reduces customer-facing ambiguity.
