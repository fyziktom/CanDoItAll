# Validate onboarding go-live readiness

**Key:** `validate-onboarding-go-live-readiness`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** customer-onboarding  
**Owner role key:** `onboarding-program-manager`  
**Gate:** go-live  
**Failure severity:** Error

## Summary
Confirms onboarding can move into go-live without relying on hidden open actions.

## Pass criteria
Critical dependencies, customer actions, and support readiness are explicit and complete enough to proceed.

## Fail criteria
Go-live is being declared based on optimism while key dependencies remain unresolved.

## Escalation rule
Escalate to customer success lead and sponsoring manager before confirming the milestone.
