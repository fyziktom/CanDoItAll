# Onboarding go-live readiness checklist

**Key:** `go-live-readiness-checklist`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** customer-onboarding  
**Owner role key:** `onboarding-program-manager`  
**Phase:** go-live

## Summary
Checks whether the customer-side and internal-side prerequisites for onboarding go-live are truly complete.

## Entry criteria
Configuration and handoff planning are largely complete and go-live is approaching.

## Exit criteria
No go-live-critical task is ownerless or hidden behind vague 'customer to confirm' language.

## Checks
- Customer-side access and ownership are explicit.
- Configuration baseline is signed off or acknowledged.
- Training or enablement artifacts are ready for affected users.
- Support handoff and escalation path are documented.

## Evidence expectations
- Configuration baseline
- Customer handoff pack
