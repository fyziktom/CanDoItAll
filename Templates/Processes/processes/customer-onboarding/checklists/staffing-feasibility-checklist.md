# Staffing feasibility checklist

**Key:** `staffing-feasibility-checklist`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** customer-onboarding  
**Owner role key:** `staffing-manager`  
**Phase:** Staffing review

## Summary
Verifies whether onboarding staffing and specialist dependencies support a credible kickoff date.

## Entry criteria
The onboarding brief exists and staffing review is starting.

## Exit criteria
A credible staffing decision exists with named gaps and fallback actions.

## Checks
- Critical specialists are identified.
- Allocation coverage is explicit for the next onboarding phase.
- Known environment or access dependencies are named.
- Any capacity gaps have an owner and escalation path.

## Evidence expectations
- Staffing readiness note.
- Named specialist list.
- Gap or fallback record.
