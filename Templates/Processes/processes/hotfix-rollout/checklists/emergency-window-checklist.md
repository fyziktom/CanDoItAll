# Emergency window checklist

**Key:** `emergency-window-checklist`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** hotfix-rollout  
**Owner role key:** `incident-commander`  
**Phase:** hotfix-window

## Summary
Ensures the emergency hotfix window has the minimum decision, communication, and rollback discipline.

## Entry criteria
A hotfix path has been chosen and the team is preparing to execute under incident conditions.

## Exit criteria
Rollback owner, communications path, watch coverage, and blast-radius framing are explicit.

## Checks
- Incident commander and hotfix specialists are assigned.
- Rollback trigger sheet is available and understood.
- Customer or stakeholder communication path is prepared.
- First-hour telemetry watch owner is assigned.

## Evidence expectations
- Rollback trigger sheet
- Hotfix watch roster
