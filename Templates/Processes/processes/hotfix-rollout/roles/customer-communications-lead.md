# Customer communications lead

**Key:** `customer-communications-lead`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** hotfix-rollout  
**Preferred executor:** person  
**Preferred project role:** CustomerContact  
**Seniority:** Senior support or customer communications leadership  
**Minimum years in primary discipline:** 5  
**Minimum years in software delivery:** 7

## Summary
Owner for externally consistent status, workaround, and resolution messaging during hotfix execution.

## Purpose
Keep external communication factual, timely, and aligned with the current operational truth.

## Staffing intent
A customer-facing crisis communicator with authority to publish incident-safe customer updates.

## Snapshot summary
Owner for externally consistent status, workaround, and resolution messaging during hotfix execution.

## Domain tags
incident-communications, customer-updates, status-management

## Knowledge requirements
- Ability to turn volatile technical status into clear external messaging without overcommitting.
- Knowledge of incident communication cadence, approval paths, and customer trust expectations.
- Understanding of what technical detail is helpful, harmful, or unsafe to disclose externally.
- Ability to maintain a single source of truth across support, account teams, and status channels.
- Knowledge of workaround and ETA messaging practices during active incidents.
- Ability to coordinate communication changes with the incident commander.

## Experience requirements
- Has handled external messaging for an incident or high-severity outage.
- Has coordinated communications with technical responders and customer-facing teams.
- Has corrected or avoided inaccurate ETA commitments under pressure.
- Has managed multi-audience incident updates with support or account teams.
- Has captured communication lessons for future incident playbooks.

## Decision rights
- Approve customer-facing incident or hotfix status language.
- Block publication of unverified technical claims or unrealistic ETAs.
- Escalate if internal facts are too weak to support promised resolution timing.
- Decide when a workaround versus full-fix message should be published.

## Owned artifacts
- Customer status update
- Incident FAQ
- External workaround note

## Collaboration expectations
- Coordinate with incident commander and customer success lead.
- Keep support teams synchronized on approved language.
- Use technical facts from responders without distorting them.
- Feed customer confusion patterns back into incident review.

## Anti-patterns
- Publishing optimism as if it were evidence.
- Letting every stakeholder improvise their own customer message.
- Sharing technical hypotheses externally as confirmed facts.
- Ignoring customer-action guidance while focusing only on root cause.

## Fitness evidence
- Approved customer updates with clear timing and ownership.
- Evidence of aligned support and customer-facing messaging.
- Reduced confusion or escalation due to communication discipline.
- Post-incident actions showing improvement of communication playbooks.
