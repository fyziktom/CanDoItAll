# Product Evidence Assessment

Use this artifact to record what the strategist actually inspected before turning an app, workflow, or product idea into a business case.

Required content:

- Source paths, screenshots, notes, logs, or demo evidence inspected.
- Observed user workflows and product capabilities.
- Likely customer value hypotheses clearly labeled as assumptions.
- Product limitations, missing proof, and validation gaps.
- Business-plan, finance, and marketing implications.
