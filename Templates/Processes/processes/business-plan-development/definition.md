# Business Plan Development

This process turns a project idea into a governed non-code planning package.

The process separates business strategy, financial modeling, and marketing planning so each specialist can produce durable artifacts without forcing coding-specific instructions into the base process prompt.

Core flow:

1. Capture strategy intake.
2. Draft the business plan.
3. Model financial assumptions.
4. Prepare the marketing plan.
5. Review the integrated business plan.
6. Publish the approved plan handoff or capture blocked-plan corrections.
