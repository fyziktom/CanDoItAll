# Containment decision note

**Key:** `containment-decision-note`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** incident-response  
**Artifact kind:** Decision  
**Owner role key:** `triage-lead`  
**Default trust requirement:** ReviewRequired  
**Default sensitivity level:** Internal  
**Default retention days:** 365

## Summary
Decision note that explains the chosen immediate containment or mitigation path during incident response.

## Allowed future usage
Reusable for diagnosis, approval escalation, and post-incident review.

## Validation requirement
Must describe the chosen containment path, expected effect, and residual uncertainty.
