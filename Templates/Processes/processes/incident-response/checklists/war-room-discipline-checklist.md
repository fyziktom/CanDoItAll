# War-room discipline checklist

**Key:** `war-room-discipline-checklist`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** incident-response  
**Owner role key:** `incident-scribe`  
**Phase:** live-response

## Summary
Checklist to keep the incident bridge factual, owned, and time-bounded under pressure.

## Entry criteria
Incident bridge is active and multiple people are contributing.

## Exit criteria
Decision owner, next checkpoint, and live action log quality remain intact.

## Checks
- Current severity and impact are stated at the top of the bridge.
- Every action has an owner and next update time.
- Status changes are timestamped.
- Speculation is labeled as speculation.

## Evidence expectations
- Live action log
- Containment plan
