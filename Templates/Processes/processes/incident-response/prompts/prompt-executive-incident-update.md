# Prompt / executive incident update

**Key:** `prompt-executive-incident-update`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** incident-response  
**Audience role key:** `triage-lead`  
**Phase:** communications

## Summary
Prompt scaffold for concise executive updates during an active incident.

## Required inputs
- customer impact
- known facts
- containment path
- next checkpoint

## Output schema
- executive summary
- business impact
- current response
- next decision horizon
- support needed

## Refusal conditions
- Refuse to include unverified root-cause claims in the executive summary.
