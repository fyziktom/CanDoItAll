# Repair brief

**Key:** `repair-brief`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** branching-code-review  
**Artifact kind:** Brief  
**Owner role key:** `review-lead`  
**Default trust requirement:** ReviewRequired  
**Default sensitivity level:** Internal  
**Default retention days:** 365

## Summary
Focused repair brief issued when the review router sends the change back for a bounded repair pass.

## Allowed future usage
Reusable between repair loops until the next routing decision is made.

## Validation requirement
Must state the repair scope, blocking findings, and what evidence is required on resubmission.
