# Component identity checklist

**Key:** `component-identity-checklist`  
**Scope:** local  
**Process:** oss-intake-supply-chain-governance  
**Owner role key:** `sbom-curator`  
**Phase:** component-intake

## Summary
Ensures the team knows exactly what component is being approved and from where.

## Entry criteria
A new component or version has been proposed.

## Exit criteria
Identity, source, and version are explicit enough for reproducible review and later response.

## Checks
- Component name, version, and upstream source are captured.
- Build or package identity is linked where available.
- Manual assumptions are documented.
- Transitive risk blind spots are called out.

## Evidence expectations
- SBOM entry
- Provenance note
