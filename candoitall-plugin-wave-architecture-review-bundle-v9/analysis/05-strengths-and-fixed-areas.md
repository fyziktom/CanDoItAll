## Strengths and genuinely improved areas
The review is not saying “nothing improved.” Several earlier problem areas look materially healthier:

- The old persisted system-managed parallel graph truth appears largely retired from the Workbench load path.
- Reparenting is cleaner and no longer looks dominated by persisted hierarchy-link truth.
- CRM/HR seams around assignment policy and node-kind capability ownership are healthier than in earlier snapshots.
- The codebase now has more obvious extension registry concepts than the earlier pre-plugin versions.

Those are real improvements and should be preserved during the next refactor wave.
