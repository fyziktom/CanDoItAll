# Corrective subbundle template

## Trigger

Describe the exact gate failure or stop-rule breach that caused this corrective subbundle.

## Allowed scope

- repair only the blocked architecture or proof problem,
- add the minimum refactor needed to restore the intended direction,
- refresh the blocked proof and rerun the failed gate.

## Forbidden scope

- no unrelated feature additions,
- no skipping the blocked gate,
- no weakening of promised proof requirements.

## Required closeout

- corrective memo,
- refreshed proof,
- rerun of the blocked gate,
- explicit pass/fail decision after the corrective.
