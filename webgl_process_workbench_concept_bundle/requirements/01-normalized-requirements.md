## Notes

- Requirements `RQ-04`, `RQ-09`, `RQ-10`, and `RQ-15` are the concept-critical design rules.
- Requirements `RQ-17` and `RQ-18` make corrective work mandatory when a gate fails.
- Requirement `RQ-23` intentionally asks for a migration rubric, not a forced product decision.
