# Execution invariants

The following invariants must remain true throughout execution:

- the universal WebGL library never references `CanDoItAll.Modules.Processes`,
- the default authoring scene remains guided, center-lane perspective 3D,
- labels remain readable through a DOM/HTML mirror or overlay,
- the sandbox remains concept-only and non-persistent,
- semantic automation exists before final proof,
- architecture gates may block all downstream work,
- representative templates always include simple + medium + dense coverage,
- screenshots complement semantic proof and never replace it for state-change assertions.
