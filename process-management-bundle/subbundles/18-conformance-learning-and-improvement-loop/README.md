## Deliverables

- Conformance observations and deviation clusters with privacy-safe handling.
- Improvement candidates and training-opportunity markers linked back to process evidence.
- Governance path from recurring issues into approved process changes, variants, or policy investigations.
- Safe non-action outcomes preserved as meaningful signals rather than treated as generic failures.

## Dependency Impact

- This is the final business-feedback loop for the module.
- If this subbundle is weak, the system will execute work but fail to improve from reality.

## Validation Depth

- `End-to-end regression and closure`

## Implementation Steps

1. Implement conformance observations, deviation reasons, and privacy controls.
2. Cluster repeated patterns from runtime history and field observations.
3. Turn repeated issues into improvement candidates and training markers.
4. Route signals into governance instead of uncontrolled self-modification.

## Scope Exceptions

- Full machine-generated process redesign is deferred; this subbundle only creates the governed learning loop.

## Do Not Do

- Do not create an unmanaged rumor log about people or teams.
- Do not treat repeated safe refusal as failure noise to discard.
- Do not let learning signals mutate live process definitions automatically.

## Acceptance Checklist

- Conformance and deviation records are explicit and privacy-aware.
- Repeated patterns become governed improvement or training candidates.
- Safe refusal and non-action stay visible as legitimate outcomes.
- Management-readable review surfaces can show paper-versus-reality gaps.
